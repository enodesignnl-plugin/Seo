W, R and Z, refined

No new concept. Three details are refined: the join in the W, the curve and taper of the R's leg, and the flag at the top left of the Z. The saffron icon, colours, typography, proportions and composition stay as they were.

Before and after

W

The hairline still runs into the heavy stroke at mid-height, but it no longer stops against it at a hard corner. Above the join a long, soft bracket carries the hairline's edge into the stroke, and below it the crotch opens along the hairline instead of closing in a small knot. There is still no crossing and no doubled weight.

R

The sweep keeps its path; only its weight changes. Before, the stroke dropped to a hairline quickly and then ran at one width. Now it thins continuously all the way to the end and finishes in a finer point. The distance to the A is unchanged at 43 units (cap height 700).

Z

The flag at the top left rises less above the cap line and hangs less far below the bar, with its curves kept intact. The diagonal is 4 units lighter, so the Z no longer reads as the darkest letter next to the A and W.
